Project Overview
GlobeTrek Travel Solutions aims to build VoyageAI, an enterprise-grade multi-agent travel assistant that
simplifies end-to-end trip planning. The platform should help users discover destinations, generate
personalized itineraries, estimate travel budgets, provide visa and travel guidance, and answer travel-related

Business Background & Problem Statement
1. Business Background
About the Client
GlobeTrek Travel Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a fast-growing travel technology company that provides digital travel
planning solutions for individual travelers, families, and corporate clients. The organization offers services
such as destination discovery, flight and hotel booking, itinerary planning, travel budgeting, visa assistance,
and travel support.

As customer expectations continue to evolve, travelers increasingly expect personalized, instant, and intelligent
travel recommendations. GlobeTrek plans to develop VoyageAI, an Agentic AI-powered travel assistant capable
of coordinating multiple specialized AI agents to provide a seamless end-to-end travel planning experience.
Current Business Environment

• Flight Booking Platform

• Travel Itinerary Planner

Although these systems provide valuable services, customers are required to switch between multiple

Why Agentic AI?
Traditional travel chatbots answer isolated questions but cannot coordinate multiple planning tasks such as
destination selection, itinerary generation, budget estimation, and travel guidance. VoyageAI adopts a

context through memory, invoke appropriate tools, and validate recommendations using a reflection stage
before presenting a complete travel plan.

Multi-Agent Architecture & System Design
9. AI Solution Architecture
VoyageAI follows a Supervisor-based Multi-Agent Architecture implemented using LangGraph. Every travel
request is first received by the Supervisor Agent, which analyzes the travel intent, identifies the required
planning activities, invokes one or more specialized travel agents, combines their outputs, validates the
complete travel plan through a Reflection Node, and returns a personalized recommendation to the traveler.
High-Level Workflow
Traveler
   |
Streamlit User Interface
   |
Supervisor Agent (LangGraph)
   |------ Destination Recommendation Agent
   |------ Itinerary Planning Agent
   |------ Budget & Booking Advisor Agent
   |------ Travel Assistance Agent
   |
Shared Conversation Memory
   |
Reflection Node
   |
Final Travel Plan

11. Memory Strategy
• Maintain traveler preferences across the conversation.
• Remember previous destinations, budgets, and travel dates.
• Reuse earlier recommendations to avoid repetitive questions.
• Share travel context among all specialized agents using LangGraph state.
12. Reflection Strategy
Before presenting the final itinerary, the Reflection Node evaluates the combined recommendations generated
by all participating agents.
• Verify that the itinerary aligns with the user's travel duration and budget.
• Check that all requested travel activities have been addressed.
• Ensure recommendations are logically ordered and complete.
• Improve clarity, consistency, and personalization of the final travel plan.
13. Tool Calling Strategy

14. Example Execution Flow
1. The traveler asks: 'Plan a 7-day trip to Japan in November with a budget of ₹2,00,000.'
2. The Supervisor Agent determines that all four specialized agents are required.
3. The Destination Recommendation Agent suggests suitable cities and attractions.
4. The Budget & Booking Advisor estimates travel costs and recommends cost-effective options.
5. The Itinerary Planning Agent generates a day-wise travel schedule.
6. The Travel Assistance Agent provides visa guidance, weather expectations, and packing tips.
7. Conversation Memory stores traveler preferences for future interactions.
8. The Reflection Node validates the complete travel plan before returning the final response.
